THE SPLIT

You pick the split.
Then it's locked.

Most launchpads lock you at 50/50. On Raftpad you pick how much of every fee floats your coin's liquidity, anywhere from 30% to 70%. Once you launch, nobody can change it, not even you.

⚿Raftpad takes 1% of the liquidity share. Your share is never touched.
HOW IT WORKS

Push off. Float. Make shore.

Three stages, one locked split, from the first trade to forever.

01

Push off on Bonk

Your coin launches on bonk.fun’s bonding curve (Raydium LaunchLab). Name, ticker, image, a raft colour and your split. Your wallet signs and stays the creator.

no presale
02

Float on the curve

Every trade pays a fee. Your share goes to you right away. The liquidity share is saved in the coin's raft fund.

split locked
03

Make shore at ~85 SOL

The coin graduates from bonk.fun to a Raydium pool, the raft fund goes in as locked liquidity, and from then on the liquidity share flows into the pool on every trade.

liquidity locked
